Two of my papers were accepted to ICLR 2026!

- 🪃 [Boomerang Distillation Enables Zero-Shot Model Size Interpolation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05064) 🪃: we uncover boomerang distillation, a surprising phenomenon by which we can create a full family of models of fine-grained sizes with no additional training by interpolating between a pretrained and distilled model.
- [Hidden Breakthroughs in Language Model Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15872): we propose POLCA, a method for decomposing changes in the loss along arbitrary bases of the low-rank training subspace, and show that POLCA can be used to find breakthroughs in training that are obscured by aggregating all variation into a single scalar loss term.
